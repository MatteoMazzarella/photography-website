ABOUT
ME.

Welcome to my photography portfolio. Driven by a deep passion for capturing the world through my lens, I turn everyday moments into visual art. Based in Switzerland—where striking landscapes meet the high-energy hub of Zurich Airport—my work finds its ultimate inspiration in the skies. From the precision and power of aircraft cutting through alpine air to the raw, unscripted pulse of urban streets, every shot tells a story. Armed with a versatile setup—from the classic feel of my Nikon D40x and the crisp performance of my Olympus OM-D to the quick, spontaneous eye of my iPhone—every frame reflects my fresh perspective, a sharp eye for detail, and an endless drive to chase the light.
